# Dinosaur Island: Wild Encounter

Play: https://dodobun7854.github.io/dino-island-play/?v=hunt2

One player, four AI opponents, roaming raptors, gunfire investigations, throwable sound lures, limited ammunition, supply crates, a shrinking zone and a T. rex escalation.

Uses the MIT-licensed YAZH player, controls, camera, weapon raycasts and BVH physics:
https://github.com/UstymUkhman/YetAnotherZombieHorror
Upstream commit: cdbde45a9911200376a449957408c3bebbaccd12

The hunt match, AI decisions and navigation, park scene, UI and synthesized audio are original adaptation work. Models are Quaternius CC0. Credits and license notices are included in THIRD-PARTY.txt and LICENSE-YAZH.txt.

Desktop keyboard/mouse, single player against AI. WASD move, Shift run, E open supplies, Q throw lure, R reload, 1/2 switch weapons, C switch view, Esc pause.

Previous three-wave prototype: waves/. Previous top-down prototype: classic/.
