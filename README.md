# Dino Island: Creature Study 06

Play: https://dodobun7854.github.io/dino-island-play/?v=creatures6
Interactive review: creatures.html . Actual captures and a 12-second silent
frame-by-frame model animation demonstration: gallery/ . Not gameplay footage.

Modified CC0 Quaternius dinosaur base, original geometry/texture adaptations and
MIT YAZH game core. Rex skull/neck, independently weighted jaw, upper/lower teeth,
gums, palate, tongue, eyes, scales and claws. Four species have refined surfaces,
embedded color/normal/roughness maps and eye detail. Raptor sickle claws, trike
frill/keratin and brachiosaurus belly surfaces differ. Original basic body forms
and main clips remain; no full foot IK.

Runtime breathing, impact settling, acceleration lean and head/tail turn response
are shared by game and review. Gait follows movement. Heavy dinosaurs turn at a
limited rate and pause in sharp reversals; raptors stay agile.

Desktop single-player last-human-standing game against four AI. Research gate,
real ramp/high ground, alarm-driven contested supply and rex breach retained.
WASD move, Shift sprint, mouse fire/aim, E facilities/supplies, Q bait, R reload,
1/2 weapons, C camera, Esc pause. Approach the front blue crate and press E.
