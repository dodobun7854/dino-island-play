# Dino Island: Research Encounter 05

Play: https://dodobun7854.github.io/dino-island-play/?v=research5
Actual captures: gallery/ . Previous cinematic teaser: promo/ .

Desktop single-player battle royale against four AI opponents. Last human alive
wins. Orange markings lead through an operable shield gate; green markings follow
the west approach. Walk up the ramp to real 2.8 m high ground. It protects against
raptor melee; the rex can reach it and humans can shoot upward.

E activates the research terminal. The alarm attracts rivals and dinosaurs. After
8 game seconds the finite high-value supply unlocks; AI can steal it. After 12
seconds the existing rex breaks out of its enclosure and approaches the station.
The rex can warn and break the service gate, while Q bait interrupts its windup.
Gate collision, weapon cover and AI routing change together. Pause freezes the
objective, actors, doors and audio. Retry resets all encounter state.

WASD move, Shift sprint, mouse aim/fire, E facilities/supplies, Q bait, R reload,
1/2 weapon, C camera, Esc pause. Release movement keys to stop immediately.
Four-view tour before Start includes the research site. First gameplay integration
of this area; early low-poly models and some earlier animations remain.
